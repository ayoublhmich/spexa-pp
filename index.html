AI transparency summary

Spexa uses AI to understand your speech, reply to your messages, and score pronunciation. Before Spexa sends your speech, typed messages, lesson answers, language settings, or learning context for AI processing, the app asks for your permission. If you do not allow it, those AI features will not send your speech or text.

When allowed, this information is processed through Spexa's secure backend and AI providers Google/Gemini and OpenAI only to provide transcription, tutor replies, pronunciation scoring, speaking feedback, and tutor audio. Spexa does not sell your personal data.

3. Collected personal data (categories & sources)

3.1. When you use the Service, we process data:

  1. you directly provide to us (for example, name, email address, your answers to onboarding questions);
  2. automatically during your interaction with the Service (for example, IP address, device language, or online activity data);
  3. we receive about you from third parties (for example, Apple ID, Google Account, or your subscription status from our payments provider).

3.2. Data directly provided by you

3.2.1. Identifiers: This may include your name, display name, and email address. You provide us with this information when you register for the Service, subscribe to our newsletters, or contact us by any other means.

3.2.2. Registration and learning profile data: You provide us with this category of information during onboarding and while using the Service. This may include your target language (the language you want to learn), your native language, your proficiency level, your learning goals, your daily study commitment and preferred study time, your interests, and any other preferences you select.

3.2.3. AI speech, text, lesson, and pronunciation data: When you use AI tutor, lesson speaking, and pronunciation features, you may provide text messages, short voice recordings, lesson answers, pronunciation attempts, selected language, language settings, and learning context. After you accept the in-app AI processing permission, this data may be sent through our backend to AI providers identified in Section 5.2 so your speech can be transcribed, your pronunciation can be scored, and tutor responses or tutor audio can be generated.

3.2.4. AI permission choice: We store whether you allowed AI processing so we can avoid asking again before the same AI data flow. If you do not allow AI processing, Spexa does not send your speech or typed messages to third-party AI providers for that feature.

3.2.5. Communication with support team: When you contact our support team, you may also provide us with some personal information.

3.3. Data we collect automatically

3.3.1. Device, region, and attribution data: We collect language settings, the country code inferred from your device locale (used to localize example names, cities, and country references inside lessons so the content feels culturally relevant), Internet Protocol address, time zone, type and model of a device, device settings, OS version, Internet service provider, mobile carrier, hardware ID, app instance identifiers, and advertising or attribution identifiers where available and permitted by your device settings. This may include App Tracking Transparency status on iOS, IDFA when permission is granted, Google Advertising ID on Android where available, AppsFlyer identifiers, campaign parameters, and attribution events connected to installs, subscriptions, and trial starts. We do not collect precise GPS location.

3.3.2. Log and usage data: We collect information on how you interact with our Service. This may include which screens you have viewed, the features and content you interact with, how often you use the Service, how long you are on the Service, your lesson progress, your streak history, and the results of your pronunciation and speaking exercises.

3.3.3. Cookies and similar technologies: Our products employ technologies (cookies, mobile SDKs, etc.) to process your data to enhance your user experience, analyze product usage, measure permitted advertising attribution, manage subscriptions, and detect crashes and errors. These technologies include Firebase Analytics (usage and retention metrics), PostHog (product analytics, feature adoption, and masked session replay), Sentry (crash and error reporting), RevenueCat (subscription status and entitlement analytics), and AppsFlyer (install and campaign attribution). On iOS, advertising tracking that requires App Tracking Transparency is controlled by Apple's native permission prompt. Disabling these technologies may affect measurement and some functionality, although our products will remain usable.
